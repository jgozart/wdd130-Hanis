Michael Jackson

Born August 29, 1958, Michael Jackson became a pop superstar and is known worldwide as the King of Pop.

He began his career as a member of The Jackson 5, performing alongside his four brothers. He later launched a solo career and quickly became one of the greatest and most influential artists of all time.

His most popular albums include Thriller, Bad, Off the Wall, and Dangerous.

Some of his biggest hit songs include Thriller, Billie Jean, and Smooth Criminal.

Michael Jackson passed away on June 25, 2009, leaving behind a massive legacy in music, dance, and global culture.
